# My Favorite Series

## Vegabond

**Vegabond** is a gripping South Korean action-thriller series about a stuntman who uncovers a deadly conspiracy after a plane crash kills his nephew. Joining forces with a secret intelligence agent, he risks everything to expose the powerful elites behind the tragedy.

### Why I Like It
- Action and triller
- Exciting story
- Truth vs. Corruption 

### Favorite Character
My favorite character Go Hae Ri because a fiercely loyal, empathic, and resouceful agent.

### Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5
